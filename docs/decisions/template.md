# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Supersedes:** —
- **Superseded by:** —

## Context

What's true that forces a decision. Facts and constraints, not opinions. If a
fact here was *measured*, say so and give the number. If it was assumed, say
that too — an assumption recorded as an assumption can be re-checked later; one
recorded as a fact cannot.

## Options considered

### Option A — name
What it is. Why it's plausible. What it costs.

### Option B — name
Same. Include the option you rejected, honestly argued. A one-sided ADR is a
press release, and it's the rejected option that future-you will want to
reconsider.

## Decision

What we chose, stated plainly.

## Consequences

What becomes easier. What becomes harder — this half matters more, and it's the
half people skip. What this forecloses. What now needs revisiting if a premise
in Context changes.

## Revisit if

The specific, checkable condition that should reopen this. "If measured RSS with
four cores resident exceeds X." Not "if it becomes a problem" — that's not a
trigger, that's a hope.
